Lynx collects real-time market data from multiple exchanges and sources, processing terabytes of information daily to identify patterns and opportunities.

About Lynx

Lynx was founded with a simple mission: to democratize access to advanced trading intelligence. We believe that everyone should have the tools to make informed financial decisions, regardless of their experience level.

Our Story

The idea for Lynx emerged from our founder's frustration with the complexity and inaccessibility of professional trading tools. After years of working in quantitative finance, Sean K set out to create a platform that would make sophisticated trading algorithms available to everyone.

Our Technology

Lynx leverages cutting-edge machine learning algorithms and real-time data processing to deliver accurate trading signals and insights. Our system continuously learns from market data to improve its predictions and adapt to changing market conditions.

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, use our services, or contact us for support. This may include your name, email address, and trading preferences.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services, to develop new ones, and to protect Lynx and our users. We also use this information to offer you tailored content.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties unless we provide users with advance notice. This does not include website hosting partners and other parties who assist us in operating our website, conducting our business, or serving our users, so long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential.

Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information when you enter, submit, or access your personal information. All sensitive information is transmitted via Secure Socket Layer (SSL) technology and then encrypted into our databases to be only accessed as stated above.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page. You are advised to review this privacy policy periodically for any changes.
